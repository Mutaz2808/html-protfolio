The Best Anime For Mutaz

My top 3 anime for all time.

1. Hunter X Hunter:

Creator by: Yoshihiro Togashi.
Hunter x Hunter is a popular Japanese manga and anime series created by Yoshihiro Togashi about a young boy named Gon Freecss who becomes a Hunter to find his father. The series follows Gon as he embarks on a journey to pass the Hunter Examination, making friends and facing dangerous challenges along the way. Known for its innovative characters, mature themes, and strategic combat, Hunter x Hunter is celebrated for its unique power system and character development, though some arcs are noted for slower pacing or unresolved plots.

2. Attack On Titan:

Creator by: Hajime Isayama.
Attack on Titan (Japanese: 進撃の巨人, Hepburn: Shingeki no Kyojin; lit. 'The Advancing Giant') is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Hajime Isayama. Set in a world where humanity is forced to live in cities surrounded by three enormous walls that protect them from gigantic man-eating humanoids referred to as Titans, the story follows Eren Yeager, an adolescent boy who vows to exterminate the Titans after they bring about the destruction of his hometown and the death of his mother. It was serialized in Kodansha's monthly magazine Bessatsu Shōnen Magazine from September 2009 to April 2021, with its chapters collected in 34 tankōbon volumes.

3. One Piece:

Creator by: Eiichiro Oda.
One Piece (stylized in all caps) is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Eiichiro Oda. It follows the adventures of Monkey D. Luffy and his crew, the Straw Hat Pirates, as he explores the Grand Line in search of the mythical treasure known as the "One Piece" to become the next King of the Pirates.
